# Code of Conduct

The `jtr` project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

## Summary

We pledge to make participation in our community a welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity. We expect contributors to engage with one another respectfully, give and accept constructive feedback in good faith, and prioritize what is best for the community as a whole.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code, please contact the project maintainers at **eb@erbiker.dev**. Reports will be reviewed promptly and treated confidentially. Reporters will not face retaliation for raising concerns in good faith.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards in the Contributor Covenant. They may take any action they deem appropriate, including warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban, in response to violations.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant — including the detailed standards, enforcement guidelines, and the four-tier enforcement ladder (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) — is the binding reference. Read it here: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
